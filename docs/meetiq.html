What Stands Out

01

Deleted, not flagged

Unverifiable content is removed before it's ever stored — nothing labeled "unverified" reaches you, because it never gets that far.

02

Two sources, cross-checked

The voice lecture and the source textbook are checked against each other, not treated as two separate, disconnected uploads.

03

Adaptive, not one-shot

A weak topic gets new quiz questions AND a new animated slide walkthrough, repeated until mastery — not a single static summary.

04

Institution-ready from day one

Approval workflow, consent tracking, audit trail, and tenant isolation are architectural, not bolted on later.
