MeetIQ — Features & Research Comparison

Every generated study material starts from one source of truth — the recorded voice lecture — and carries a verified trust score all the way through. Here's the full feature set, what stands out, and how it measures up against existing research.

What Stands Out

01

Deleted, not flagged

Most tools show a disclaimer next to shaky content. MeetIQ removes what it can't verify before it's ever stored — there is nothing labeled "unverified" to see, because it never reaches you.

02

Confidence follows the whole chain

From raw speech recognition through every note, flashcard, and quiz answer — not just a transcript-level disclaimer, a score on each individual item.

03

Adaptive, not one-shot

A wrong topic gets new questions AND a new animated slide explanation, repeated until mastery — not a single static summary you either get or don't.

04

Institution-ready from day one

Faculty approval workflow, recording consent, audit trail, and tenant isolation are built into the architecture, not bolted on after launch.
